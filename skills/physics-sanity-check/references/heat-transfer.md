# Heat-Transfer Physics Sanity Reference

Use when heat transfer is active.

Check:
- heat source vs temperature maxima
- direction of temperature gradient
- conduction path
- convective heat removal
- thermal boundary layers
- energy balance
- heat flux sign
- radiation importance where relevant
- Nusselt / Reynolds trend consistency

Potential concerns:
- temperature maximum far from all heat sources without explanation
- heat flux direction contradicting temperature gradient
- energy output exceeding input without storage/source explanation
- compared cases using inconsistent colour scales
