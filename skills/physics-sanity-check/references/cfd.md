# CFD Physics Sanity Reference

Use only for CFD studies.

## Flow-field checks
- stagnation point location
- acceleration around constrictions / edges
- separation onset
- reattachment
- recirculation
- wake development
- pressure recovery
- reverse flow
- mass conservation
- symmetry / asymmetry
- near-wall behaviour

## Potential red flags
- streamline penetration of solid walls
- sharp discontinuity without physical interface
- pressure/velocity patterns contradicting basic flow response
- recirculation appearing in implausible locations without geometric cause
- outlet boundary dominating internal flow
- field patterns aligned suspiciously with mesh blocks
- identical contours despite materially different boundary conditions

## Caution
A surprising result is not automatically wrong.
Unexpected behaviour may arise from:
- 3D effects
- secondary flow
- buoyancy
- unsteady flow
- roughness
- local separation
- coupling with heat / particles

Ask for discriminating evidence before judging.
